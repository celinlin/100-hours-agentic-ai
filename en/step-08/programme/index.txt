<li>What is an irreversible action?</li>
<li>What is the difference between a workflow and an agent?</li>
<li>What does "human in the loop" mean?</li>
<li>Why not let the agent do everything?</li>
<li>Where should the rule go, in the instructions or in the code?</li>
<li>What should the agent do after a refusal?</li>
<li>What is least privilege?</li>
<li>How do you prove that nothing goes out on its own?</li>
</ol><aside class="margin margin--time"><span class="margin__time">1 h 30</span><span class="margin__note">One resource, one question.</span></aside><h3 id="seance-1-2" data-activity="seance-1-2" data-minutes="90" data-time="1 h 30" data-kind="learn">Learn</h3><p>Move on to the next resource as soon as you can say the sentence given.</p><div class="resource"><span class="resource__time">10 min</span><h4 class="resource__q">Workflow or agent?</h4><p><a href="https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer" class="ext" data-host="anthropic.com">Building effective agents<span class="sr-only"> (opens in a new tab)</span></a>, Anthropic, 2024.
